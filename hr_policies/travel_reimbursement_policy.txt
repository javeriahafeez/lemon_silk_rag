LEMON SILK TECHNOLOGIES
TRAVEL AND REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Status: Approved
Effective Date: 1 January 2026

Business travel must be approved by the employee's manager before booking.

Employees should use company-approved travel and accommodation options where available.

Reasonable meal expenses incurred during approved business travel are reimbursable.

Receipts are required for reimbursement claims.

Employees should submit travel expense claims through the company's expense system within 15 days of completing the trip.
