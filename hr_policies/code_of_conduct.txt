LEMON SILK TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE CODE OF CONDUCT

Status: Approved
Effective Date: 1 January 2026

Employees must treat colleagues, customers and business partners with respect and professionalism.

Confidential company information must not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals.

Employees must disclose potential conflicts of interest to their manager or the appropriate company representative.

Company systems and information should be used responsibly and in accordance with company policies.

Employees should report suspected misconduct through the company's approved reporting channels.
