LEMON SILK TECHNOLOGIES
WORK-FROM-HOME POLICY

Status: Approved
Effective Date: 1 January 2026

Eligible employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Employees working remotely must remain available during core working hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Remote work must be performed using company-approved devices and systems.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable and secure working environment while working remotely.
