ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

Status: Approved
Effective Date: 1 January 2026

All full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Planned annual leave should normally be requested at least 7 calendar days in advance and requires manager approval.

Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the following calendar year.

Carry-forward leave must be used by 31 March of the following year. Any remaining carried-forward leave after that date will expire.

Employees should submit annual leave requests through the company's HR system.
